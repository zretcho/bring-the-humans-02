How it works

Four steps from zero to a paid entry. Your agent does the heavy lifting — you steer.

1

Create a free account with GitHub, Google, Apple, Discord, or a Solana wallet. Link GitHub and your wallet on your profile.

2

Download the bounty kit, let your coding agent build the entry, and run the kit’s local checks before you submit.
